# Eagles Healthcare NEMT Transport Platform

Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT) dispatch, driver workflow, and patient transfer platform for **Eagles Healthcare Patient Transfer Services**.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Live CAD Dispatch Command Center & Telematics Map**
   - Real-time GPS fleet tracking across metropolitan medical sectors (Emory, Piedmont, Grady, Fresenius, DaVita, VA).
   - Automated wheelchair & stretcher capacity matching algorithm.
   - Interactive trip pipeline (Scheduled, Assigned, En Route, Arrived, In Transit, Completed).
   - Simulated live vehicle telemetry movements and emergency hospital discharge injector.

2. **Patient & Clinic Intake Booking Engine**
   - Comprehensive intake form supporting standard ambulatory, wheelchair (standard & bariatric), and gurney/stretcher transfers.
   - Automated HCPCS billing rate calculations (T2003 base, A0428 BLS stretcher, S0215 mileage).
   - Standing recurring appointments (Dialysis M/W/F, Oncology cycles).

3. **Driver In-Cab Mobile Data Terminal (MDT)**
   - Manifest run-sheet with turn-by-turn workflow milestones.
   - Built-in HTML5 Canvas Digital Signature Pad for patient and nurse electronic proof of delivery.
   - Pre-trip ADA hydraulic lift & Q'Straint tie-down safety inspections.
   - MDT 10-33 panic alert.

4. **Fleet & ADA Asset Registry**
   - Vehicle equipment tracking (BraunAbility hydraulic lifts, Stryker Power-LOAD cots, oxygen tanks, AEDs).
   - Safety certification dates, fuel levels, and odometer telematics.

5. **Medicaid & CMS-1500 Billing Clearinghouse**
   - HCPCS fee schedule calculations, claim status tracking, and EDI 837P / CSV claims export.

6. **Patient & Family Live Tracker**
   - Real-time ETA countdown, vehicle license plate identification, and driver contact portal.
